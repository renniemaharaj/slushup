# Website policies

Five short policies are maintained in `src/lib/content/policies.ts` and prerendered through `src/routes/[policy]/`. The shared renderer and footer use the same registry, keeping labels and navigation consistent. Contact details come from `profile.ts`.

The content describes the actual static website. It distinguishes application code without tracking or browser storage from hosting-platform security/access cookies and logs. It does not introduce a consent banner, tracking, a reporting backend, a bug bounty, response-time promises, fixed refund schedules, or an automatic service contract.

Project scope, fees, payment, ownership, cancellation, and ongoing support are left to a written engagement agreement and applicable law. The policies are generic website terms and do not assert compliance certification or replace project-specific terms.

Background references reviewed on 16 November 2026:

- [TT-CSIRT: General Privacy Principles](https://ttcsirt.gov.tt/data-protection/): purpose limitation, necessary collection/retention, safeguards, access and correction.
- [Trinidad and Tobago Parliament: Data Protection Act, 2011](https://www.ttparliament.org/publication/the-data-protection-act-2011/): local legislative source; no claim is made here about the commencement of every provision.
- [ICO: Cookies and similar technologies](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/cookies-and-similar-technologies/): reference for distinguishing optional tracking from necessary access/security storage, not an assertion that UK law governs every visitor or engagement.
